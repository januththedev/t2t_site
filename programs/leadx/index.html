Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common queries regarding the Lead-x program structure and expectations.

Is this only for management students?

No. The program is suitable for students and graduates from business, management, IT, computer science, finance, HR, marketing, engineering, logistics, hospitality, and other general backgrounds. Any student who wants to transition successfully to corporate leadership is encouraged to apply.

Do I need prior AI or data knowledge?

No. The program starts with practical foundations. We do not teach coding or machine learning algorithms; instead, we focus on how managers use data dashboards and AI tools in real business situations to speed up operations and draft quality deliverables.

Is this a traditional lecture-based course?

No. Traditional courses focus on passive learning. T2T Lead-x runs on an active studio model: interactive workshops, weekly practical assignments, mentorship critiques, and a final Boardroom Challenge simulating an executive board proposal.

Will this guarantee a management trainee job?

No program can guarantee a job. However, T2T Lead-x is designed to significantly improve your readiness, confidence, and employability for management trainee and executive-track roles. You will exit the program with a portfolio and corporate presentation experience that sets you far ahead of general applicants.

What is the fee?

The fee is LKR 10,000 per month for 3 months, totaling LKR 30,000 for the initial core training. The full Lead-x journey extends through 20 weeks with an 8-week real business challenge, and top performers may be offered a paid internship after the first 3 months.

Who should apply?

Final-year undergraduates, fresh graduates, early-career professionals (0-2 years of experience), and anyone preparing for management trainee or future leadership selection processes.
