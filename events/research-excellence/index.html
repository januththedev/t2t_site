Why this workshop?

Every year, many students start research projects with enthusiasm but struggle due to unclear topics, weak research gaps, unrealistic plans, or loss of motivation. These challenges are common and preventable. This workshop addresses the real, often unspoken issues that derail research early on and provides practical guidance to help you define a clear direction, plan realistically, and stay motivated so you can progress confidently and complete your research successfully.

Common challenges we see:

  • Identifying research gaps and formulating a research-worthy problem/issue and questions
  • Devising a realistic research plan
  • Losing the initial enthusiasm
  • Building a positive relationship with research supervisor/s and other stakeholders

What Makes This Workshop Different

This is not a traditional “research methodology” session.

  • This face-to-face interactive session aims to ensure that participants leave with a tentative research problem and a realistic plan
  • Builds on real experiences of the facilitators as academics/researchers, supervisors and examiners
  • Focuses on decisions, trade-offs, and mistakes
  • Provides practical guidance
  • Relevant for both full-time/part time students and working professionals

With extensive experience in supervising, mentoring, and examining postgraduate researchers over decades, and with deep insight into research progression, completion and publishing. They have also won numerous awards nationally and internationally for research excellence.

Who should attend

This workshop is ideal for management research students/researchers, specifically:

  • Doctoral students (PhD, DBA)
  • MBA, MSc, or MPhil students
  • Final-year undergraduates research students
  • Working professionals planning postgraduate research
  • Researchers who are struggling to progress or complete on time

What the Workshop Covers

This workshop will guide participants through the essential foundations and practical realities of completing high-quality management research, including:

  • Identifying research gaps and formulating a research-worthy problem and questions in business/management research
  • Devising a realistic research plan and executing a high-quality study
  • Maintaining initial enthusiasm and sustaining research momentum
  • Building a constructive and positive relationship with research supervisors and key stakeholders
